Tiger Motors · Memories & testimonials

Voices from the Lab

About fifteen years ago, Tiger Motors was an empty space with a gravel floor. Piece by piece, through donations, grants and a lot of student work, it became a full Lean manufacturing lab. This page collects its story and what the people who learned in it have said since.
